Controls

        

            
Touch
put a finger on the sky: your dragon turns toward it · lift to fly straight · bolt button (or a second finger) to boost · chevrons to brake · flame for dragonfire · ❚❚ to pause · speaker to mute

            
Mouse
steer — the further from centre, the harder you turn (Mouse sensitivity in Settings → Player)

            
Click / W / Space
boost (burns length)

            
Right-click / S
brake — tighter turns

            
A / D
roll

            
Arrow keys
steer without the mouse

            
C
look back (also: wide view & mirror in Settings → Player)

            
Body temp
the sun at the centre and crowds warm you up — hotter = faster, with a bigger thrust tank

            
Thrust
boost runs on a ~10 s tank that refills when you're not boosting

            
E / F
breathe — grab a crystal first: red fire burns through a tail, blue ice slows everyone in the cone, violet lightning stuns a line of dragons, green acid spits a cloud that melts tails. Crystals stack (up to 5): one breath spends them all, reaching farther and wider, and the last colour you ate decides what you breathe

            
⚔ Fight mode
on servers marked ⚔ Fight (and in Settings → Server for solo): bite any part of another dragon to break off the rest, and bodies are safe to touch. Head to head, a dragon a quarter bigger wins; closer in size, you both bounce off. A violet Poison Skin crystal makes your body poisonous for 10 s: nobody can bite you, and a head that touches it is out

            
Esc / P
pause — online, folds your wings: after 3 s you leave the sky and your length is kept

            
N
music on / off

            
M
mute / unmute all sound (or the speaker button, top left)

            
`
tuning panel (the key left of 1)
